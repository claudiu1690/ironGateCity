# Review 4: the user's notes after slice 5 (6 Oct 2026)

Played on the local build after slice 5 (commit `a9e7390`). This is the **first part** of the review; more notes follow.

## Notes (verbatim, lightly tidied)

1. Portrait faces for character creation look the same: a couple have basically the same face. We don't want that; generate distinct ones.
2. The story at the start with the old man and the questions is really bad. We need another introduction; give me 3–5 examples. I choose a party and then I have party orders. We need a better intro story.
3. The paper looks good in portrait and landscape, but on a large desktop monitor it feels a bit odd. We might need a border or something.
4. A lot of things are time-dependent but there's no clock anywhere. We should have a clock somewhere.
5. After choosing to spend the level-up point in the mission modal, it still says *Level 2 · place your point*.
6. After completing the party orders, the mission says *Order carried out · +20 Party XP. That's all three: see the note.* What note? Confusing.
7. Going from one city to another should happen on the nation map: a pin that moves from X to Y along the roads, with encounters happening at random as the pin moves.
8. Irongate: parts of the city are not in any district. The districts should cover ALL of the city map.
9. In Irongate I can register in a district or take a room for 7 days. What is this? Why does it matter? Why take a room vs register as home, or why register at all?
10. I won a council seat. Now what? Is there something I can do now?
11. I went to the council, but it's just *Reading Room Grant*, already passed. The whole system is confusing: are these laws, buffs? If active, they need to be visible in the city. We need a point on the map (the party HQ or similar) where we can see the council members, the laws for the city, etc. Or a better idea.
12. How do I get Heat? What does it do? I have Heat 60 and it seems to do nothing. Heat has no tooltip and is very small in the top bar; I didn't see it at first. Clicking Political Capital shows the Heat tooltip.
13. In a different city, clicking the home city's orders does nothing. Maybe we shouldn't show them while in another city.
14. In Irongate's Places there's an *ISSUE* tag; I don't know what it is.
15. Prison: I like *"Name." He has it already. "Address." He has that too. He writes slowly, because he can.*
16. *You can close the game. The door opens at 03:59 either way, and Energy keeps refilling in here.* Let's not say that; we don't want the player to close the game while in prison.
17. *Legal Aid: half price*: what does it mean?
18. What does Reputation actually do in the city?
19. Idea: a list of every player in prison at the time.
20. Are there no repercussions for prison? I paid bail and only lost Heat and 40 Iron.
21. Back in my home city there are odd outlines, like districts. Why do home cities have districts? Only Irongate has districts. Locked points can be greyed out with a lock or tooltip.
22. In my home city an order says *Take on a mission anywhere in Ashford*, but I can't find any missions.
23. Design question: should there be a paper for each city, so that in another city you get that city's paper?
24. The Health bar isn't displayed at all times. Why?
25. Show the ranks' names along with Party XP, and what each rank unlocks.
