# Review 5: the user's notes after the review-4 fixes (8 Oct 2026)

Notion: **Design Review 2**. Played on the local build at commit `f9096bd`, which has slices 0–5 and every fix from Design Review 1.

## Notes (verbatim, lightly tidied)

1. The dialogue and story with the three people on the platform is weak. We need something stronger. Who am I? Why am I at the station? Stuff like this. The three people also need something specific, and it doesn't make sense.
2. The small clouds look odd. We should have bigger clouds.
3. The boxes for the level-up stats aren't user-friendly: big black sharp-cornered boxes. I don't know about this one.
4. There's a scroll bar on some missions when I level up. It isn't styled and looks out of place.
5. I got a job at the factory for 216 a day, but I found a job that needs AGI and Level 3 or so, paying 200 a day. This doesn't make sense. Check all the jobs.
6. Health should stay under Energy.
7. I could skip the train encounter by clicking something else. This shouldn't happen.
8. I should be able to heal at any infirmary for some money.
9. In a different city I should have a list of the Issues and missions available there, so I don't have to search every place.
10. We need to put emphasis on the danger and importance of doing things in a rival city. Maybe a bar at the top showing how we can influence the city, or a higher goal to achieve there.
11. I don't know if time should pass in real time. We need the day/night cycle more often. Investigate this and tell me if it's a good idea.
12. At night, should there be different missions? Being on rival ground at night: if you're an Alliance member in the Collective's town, there would be patrols and so on, and you could have encounters just by being in the city doing nothing. Tell me what you think.
