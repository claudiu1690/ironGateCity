# Review 2 — the user's second play-through (30 Sep 2026)

Played after the review-1 fixes (`d2e9470`), on desktop and on a phone (portrait and landscape).

## Notes (verbatim)

- Using terms like leaflets or canvass: the words are too complicated. We need simple words for those.
- When I click a location in the city, open it in the middle of the screen on desktop. Having it on the side is bad, because once I click a location I don't care what's on the map any more.
- The landscape version for mobile is completely unplayable: the map is too small, everything opens too big, everything is scrollable, I can move the map. It's really bad.
- Remove "CHA 7 and INT 8 average 7.5, 0.5 below the 8 this needs: 48 %, and +10 % for your first day in Coalport: 58 %. Rolled 11: Success (58 or under)." from the missions. Nobody cares. There is a roll; we care whether it's a success or a partial, but we don't need the details. If it's not a success, we can write why it's not a success. But don't show the rolls.
- PC is confusing. It needs to say Political Capital.
- I voted in the council poll, and it's over. Nothing happened, I can't see anything, I don't know where those names are. I also registered for something; nothing happened. I don't understand this feature at all. Either it's extremely confusing or it's not working.
- I need to see the progress on the Faction XP bar.
- The map is extremely shifty: I can move it left, right, anywhere. I should be able to do that ONLY when I'm zoomed in on a location.
- I shouldn't be able to zoom in freely on the map. Once I click a location, I should be zoomed into that location with a smooth animation.
