# Review 3 — the user's notes after the new maps (2 Oct 2026)

Played on the local build after the maps v3 work (tiles, the map filling the screen, the Places list, the clouds).

## Notes (verbatim, lightly tidied)

- Let the user zoom in on the city map if he wants.
- When you click on a point on the map, the overlay and the modal with the details need to come up smoothly. Right now it pops. Make some nice tween effect.
- The mission completion screen has those bulky squares for XP that are more than half empty. It doesn't look good. Can we fix that design?
- The Again buttons: I don't really like them. It says "Again ×3 24". I know 24 is the Energy, but it looks confusing.
- There are training actions, but at the Union Hall for Intelligence it just says TRAIN. It sounds odd: train your intelligence? This is not a gym.
- The election screen is really bad: it is just over a beige background. Can't we make it the background of the building in the city that is important for elections? Also the election system doesn't really make sense. Are those NPCs? Are those players? How does it work? It's very confusing.
