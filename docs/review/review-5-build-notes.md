**Start a new account** to see the new opening. An account made before this build keeps its character, and a sign-up that stopped half-way through the old story picks up at the beat it reached, with the new words.

## Dev panel shortcuts added this round

- **To dusk**: a minute past the next dusk. The **Night** row shows whether it is night, the next dusk and dawn, and a tap's run-in chance where you stand.
- **Patrol: Next / Never / Auto**: *Next* makes the next look at a rival town at night a stop; *Auto* is the game's own chance (12 % a look, at most twice a night).
- **Push it into Unrest**: the rival town chosen in *Take me to* falls into Unrest in your name (1.8 points pushed). **Next day** pays the prize.

## Words we chose that you may want to change

A few lines had no wording in the design, so we wrote them; say if any jar:

- on a phone the rival plate's goal is one line, *The goal: Unrest · 5.0 points to go · your party has pushed 0.4 this week* or *Duskwall is in Unrest · hold it · 8.0 points above the floor*; the whole sentence you approved shows from 640 px wide and opens the Unrest note at every size (on a phone the full sentence covered most of the town's pins);
- the danger line is one line on a phone and cuts off after the risk word (*Risk here: Risky · every tap can bring …*); its note says the rest;
- *What's here* when nothing points to a town: *No order points here today*; with no Issue: *No Issue this week*;
- the counter's button before you pick someone: *Choose one of the three*.

## Things to look at while you play

- **Rival ground** no longer shows its own Heat line on the plate: the risk word carries it, and Heat is in the top bar.
- **The prize** is paid at the day's turn, not the moment the town tips: a town that falls under 60 and drifts back over it before the turn pays nobody. A wobble at the line within a week pays nobody twice.
- **Legacy +2** for the prize waits for slice 8 (Legacy is not built yet).
- **The paper** names your party's top three pushers in the prize headline; with only one name, two of the papers' decks read a little oddly (*Ottilie Brandt are named by the district office*).
- A night mission's Iron line shows its *Night rate* part under **Details**; a Failure pays no Iron, so it carries no night tag.
- The automated tests run the town held at day (they switch night on when they need it); your local game follows the real clock.
