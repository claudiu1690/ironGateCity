# Review 6: what changed in the build (15 Oct 2026)

Your three notes from Design Review 3 (Notion), one by one: what the game does now, and where to look. Everything below is in the local build on branch `slice-0`. Start it with `pnpm dev:mem` and open <http://localhost:5173>. The dev panel (the dashed **Dev** button) gives Level 10, which opens the nation map and the train.

| # | Your note | What the build does now | Where to see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | On a small phone held sideways I can't scroll the nation map to see Clearwater or Ashford | **Every city can be dragged fully into view**, its pin and its label, clear of the caption and the buttons. The cause was the page, not the drag: the nation screen kept a 420 px minimum height, so on a short screen held sideways the map ran past the bottom of the screen, and the drag stopped with the southern cities in that hidden strip. The map now takes the room the screen has, as the city screen already did. The map stays locked, with the same zoom and limits. Checked at 667 × 375, 568 × 320, 844 × 390 and 932 × 430, and upright at 375 × 667 and 390 × 844, where nothing changed. | Level 10, the Map tab, **Nation**, phone held sideways. | R6-1 | **The journey screen held sideways** kept a 420 px minimum height, so on a short phone its map ran below the screen | **Held sideways, the journey's plate is a 260 px column on the left and the map fills the rest, the full height of the screen** (as the city screen's column). Measured: at 667 × 375 the map is 343 × 328 px and ends at the screen's bottom (before: it ran past it); at 568 × 320, 244 × 273. Your pin and its label are on the map and on the screen at all four sideways sizes. **The whole trail is in view at 667 × 375 and 568 × 320**; at 844 × 390 and 932 × 430 (wide but short) 93–94 % of it is, with the start behind your pin a drag away, because the map keeps two cities' pins from overlapping (review 3's rule) rather than zooming out further; the trail around your pin and ahead of it is always in view. Upright phones (plate on top) and desktops (the 480 px side sheet) are unchanged. | Board a train (Level 10), phone held sideways. | R6-2 | From 640 to about 700 px wide the city's 420 px corner card and the two map buttons were close | **Measured, they overlapped**: the *What's here* + *City · Nation* row (269 px) ran under the card by 44 px at 640 wide, 42 px at 667 and 9 px at 700; at 720 there was an 11 px gap, at 768 59 px. **From 640 to 767 px the two buttons now stack** in the top right corner, *What's here* over *City · Nation*, 8 px apart, so the gap to the card is 64 px at 640, 91 at 667, 124 at 700 and 144 at 720, upright and sideways (taller than 500 px) alike. From 768 px they stay in one row (59 px gap). On a desktop the + / − buttons move down under the stack in that range. Short phones held sideways have no corner card (the side column) and are unchanged. | Any city map on a small tablet. Pictures: `review-6/before-city-640x900-card.png` → `review-6/after-city-640x900-card.png` |
| — | **The dev clock reset on every save** (`pnpm dev:mem` reloads the API, and the clock's offset lived only in memory), so a character settled on a skipped-ahead day got *Too many things at once* (409) on every act | **The clock's offset is kept in the database** (dev and memory mode only) and read back when the API starts, so the clock never goes back after a reload. It still only moves forward. Stopping the whole `pnpm dev:mem` resets it with its database; each Playwright run has a fresh database, so nothing carries over. **If a character is ever ahead of the clock anyway, an act now says so at once** (*Dev clock is behind this character: settled on day N … skip the clock forward*) instead of retrying to 409; reading a screen still works. It is not treated as settled: every act is guarded on "settled today" and the day's counters belong to the later day, so that would have meant loosening every act's guard. In production (no dev hooks) the same case, a second's clock skew between servers at midnight, gets the usual *Tap again* at once. | Dev panel, *Next day*, then save any file: the clock stays |
| — | **The trail at night** | **Checked: legible, no change.** On the night art the paper ring around each ink dot reads as a row of small light rings, clear of the lamp-lit towns (the dot itself merges with the dark ground); the pin and labels are unaffected. | Picture: `review-6/before-journey-667x375-night-trail.png` (same as `after-…`) |
