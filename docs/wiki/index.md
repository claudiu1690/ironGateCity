# Index — the wiki catalog

Every page, by folder, with one line on what it covers. **Read this first** on any question, then
open the pages. Rules: [[CLAUDE|operating manual]]. Start here if you're new: [[overview]].

## Systems — character and resources

- [[Time and the Game Day]] — Real-time City Day at 00:00 UTC, settled lazily; six-hour town day with night in the last two hours.
- [[The Morning Paper]] — First screen each day: one paper per player, local editions, a stored edition plus live political rows.
- [[Session Loop]] — The five-to-ten-minute loop, agendas, archetypes, the first week, the Today tally, what being away costs.
- [[Energy]] — Max 100, +5 every 10 min (full in 3h20); spent on actions and training, never jobs or travel.
- [[Health]] — 100 + 5 a level, +10 every 10 min; lost only in run-ins; at 0 the infirmary.
- [[Rested]] — Full-bar overflow, cap 200 at the residence; +50 % XP and Iron per Energy point, never Party XP.
- [[Heat and Record]] — Heat 0–100 cools 5 an hour with four states; the caught check, marks, record levels, clearing a record.
- [[Political Capital]] — Earned by political work, capped at 1,000, never bought; spent on standing, backing, striking marks.
- [[Iron Marks and the Economy]] — Income above cost, no upkeep; earned from actions, missions and wages; optional sinks, built and planned.
- [[Buffs]] — Well-Fed and In Good Spirits are MVP slice 8, not built yet; Lodged is replaced by the home; Weatherproof waits for weather.
- [[Levels and Faction Rank]] — XP levels open content, Party XP ranks open political rights; level-up cards, reward rates per tier.
- [[Stats and Checks]] — Three trained stats plus worn Charisma; the one check formula, shown to players only as odds words; training.
- [[Jobs]] — A wage paid at the day boundary, seniority up to +20 %, a 14-day cap, the 'asks more, pays more' ladder.
- [[Coming Home]] — The three-beat demob-office opening that sets party, stats and Ambition; home city; eight taps to the first result.
- [[The Result Modal]] — One modal per action: stamp, reason line, receipt, three knock-ons, buttons that name their cost; server sends the breakdown.

## Systems — street play

- [[Street Actions]] — Tier 1: one-tap tickets (talk to voters, posters, speech, watch and listen, party meeting, training), ×1 or ×3; slice 1.
- [[Missions]] — Tier 2: a briefing and two or three ways, risk in words, check → run-in → caught check; 26 built, night windows.
- [[Local Standing]] — Reputation per city: Stranger to One of Us at 10/30/70/150 wins, +3 % a level, never decays.
- [[Party Orders]] — Three daily orders from the party secretary (welcome set, capital rotation, off in the cells); the Chair's orders are slice 7.
- [[The Dossier]] — Intelligence entries on named people, Case Files, the exposé, sale and informants; slice 8, not built.
- [[Bodyguards]] — Prepaid 7-day protection contracts (GDD §12); MVP, slice 8, not built yet; prices and shop are App C #110.
- [[Run-ins and Combat]] — NPC-only, one choice (Fight, Run, Talk, Pay, luck); the held result, settled by luck after 5 min; slice 5.
- [[Hospital and Treatment]] — The infirmary after a lost run-in (30 Health, 20 min) and Get patched up at 1 Iron a point.
- [[Jail and the Cells]] — Arrest, a sentence of 20 + Heat ÷ 2 min, bail, orders off, always a mark, the In the cells now list.
- [[Bars]] — Bar places with tier-1 tickets are built; bar services, their buffs and the back room's mark bribe are MVP slice 8, not built yet.
- [[Equipment and Wardrobe]] — Built: two slots, starting kit, keepsakes, Charisma from what you wear. Slice 8: the four-slot Wardrobe, outfits, shops.
- [[Night and Patrols]] — Two-hour nights, night-only ways and missions (+25 % Iron), random patrol stops on rival ground; review 5.
- [[Ambitions]] — Three 12-chapter personal stories, at most one chapter a week, never failing; chapters 1–2 partly built.
- [[Patronage and Favours]] — Patrons Crane and Marr (slice 8), favours between party mates (slice 6), the welcome favour at the HQ (built).
- [[Legacy]] — A permanent public record of political deeds plus eight small lasting perks; MVP, slice 8, not built yet.
- [[Residence]] — Built: the political home (500 Iron, Rank 2, 7 days between moves) and Irongate lodgings. Slice 8: the room and the flat.

## Systems — the city and the map

- [[Influence and Opinion]] — City and district opinion meters, how a swing is drawn, floors, drift, damping; control grants access, not multipliers.
- [[Battleground and Groundswell]] — Close race (+25 %) and comeback bonuses, home-city morale, the review-5 push ring and the Unrest prize.
- [[Influence Ledger and City Hero]] — A ledger row per opinion change, the plate's rank line, City and District Heroes; the slice-6 scoreboard.
- [[Issues of the Week]] — Two Issues per city each Monday: +50 % on tagged work, momentum, Sunday ownership, week-long effects.
- [[Weather]] — Per-city daily weather shifting odds ±10 %, never blocking; designed, not built, and no slice schedules it.
- [[National Control]] — Each party's weighted share across the cities (40/30/10/10/10); half the national score. Slice 7: built (the snapshot, the bar and law chips over the nation map).
- [[Capital Districts]] — Irongate's five districts: own meters, states, two seats and Issues; the outlines tile the picture.
- [[Travel and Journeys]] — Timed trains from Level 10, third class 20 Iron, lazy arrival, journey cards, the route drawn on the map.
- [[Home Cities]] — A party's home never drops below 50 %; morale, the rival-pressure cap, one map with locked pins.
- [[Hostile Ground]] — Rival home cities: harder checks, ×1 only, 15 % run-ins, Heat ×2, more Party XP, deportation; the rival header.
- [[Quarters and the Map]] — One painted picture per city, AVIF tiles, quarters open at Levels 1/6/10, the Town Hall, What's here.

## Systems — politics, factions and seasons

- [[Offices]] — The office ladder (councillor → Governor → Chair → deputy → President), pay and gates; only councillor is built.
- [[City Council and the Rule Vote]] — Towns vote every five days on one of three rule cards; seats by reputation plus backers. Reworked 12 Oct.
- [[Ordinances]] — The eleven bounded council rules, the closed effects language, and how the rule vote picks the one in force.
- [[National Elections]] — Each 28-day term elects a President and Parliament's 60 seats by D'Hondt. Built in slice 7.
- [[The Legislature and Laws]] — Parliament's bills, votes and veto; 13 bounded laws as a third modifier source. Built in slice 7 (in Unrest the locals vote the secretary's line).
- [[Ministers]] — Weekly bounded settings per ministry; post-MVP and parked, with little design detail.
- [[Recall and Removal]] — Petitions to dissolve a council, votes to remove the President or the Chair; no snap elections. Built in slice 7.
- [[NPC Fill]] — Local candidates, secretaries, local deputies and party figures fill every list; councils built, national slice 7.
- [[Chat and Presence]] — Party chat channels, 'here now' presence, bail/visit/nudge favours. Slice 6, built.
- [[Campaign Events]] — Co-op events: signing up is the commitment, volunteers fill empty roles, results by modal. Slice 6, built.
- [[Political Seasons]] — 84-day seasons, the season's score, the Champion and awards, the rollover. Slice 8, designed only.
- [[Monetisation]] — Energy packs (120 a day cap), Premium, the never-for-sale list, the Faction Reset token. GDD only, no slice.
- [[Moderation and Fair Play]] — Sign-up caps, linked accounts counted once, the audit, reports, filter, sanctions, content policy. Slice 9.
