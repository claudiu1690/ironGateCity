# Irongate City — the wiki

**Irongate City** is a political browser RPG set in a fictional 1946 Central European republic.
Three factions — the [[Iron Vanguard]], the [[Red Collective]] and the [[Civic Alliance]] — compete
for five cities through canvassing, rallies, espionage and elections. Players who win office pass
rules and laws that change the game's numbers for everyone. It is built for **short sessions**: a
few minutes, several times a day, one tap or one choice at a time, and being away costs
opportunity, never assets ([[GDD#1.2 Design pillars|GDD §1.2]]).

This wiki is the **map** of the project: how each system works in plain words, where its rules and
code live, what is built, and what was decided. The [[GDD]] stays the source of truth for every
rule and number. How the wiki is kept: [[CLAUDE|operating manual]]. Full catalog: [[index]].
