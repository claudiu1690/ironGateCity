Irongate City · Art & interface direction · proposal 01

Survey & Noir

Maps drawn like 1940s surveys. People printed like noir. The interface is printed matter.

For review · not final art Open the playable mockups
01

Maps you want to lean into

Every map is a detailed pen-and-watercolour survey: each building drawn with its windows and chimneys, trams in the streets, barges on the river. Portraits and posters stay bold graphic noir, because they are a different kind of object.

02

A printed interface

Every panel is a printed object from 1946: the newspaper, the index card, the enamel street sign, the tram ticket, the rubber stamp. No glass, no glow, no fantasy frames. Square corners and ink rules.

03

Politics is the colour

The art stays neutral. Faction colour lives only in the interface: plates, bars, stamps, badges. A city can change hands overnight without repainting a single map.

The republic, by day and by night

Map 1 of 6 · the national view

Day plate · 06:00–19:00. The whole country in one view: mountains, forests, farmland, the coast. The five cities are small and far apart, joined by long rail lines, so a 12–25 minute journey looks like one.

Night plate · 19:00–06:00. Lit windows, furnace glow in Coalport, the searchlight at Duskwall. Evening sessions, when Campaign Events happen, feel different.

Four cities, four characters

Maps 3–6 · same camera, same inks

Ashford · the domed library and quad, the columned law courts, the newspaper and press club, the canal. Calm and respectable.

Coalport · the steel mill, coal yards, the docks and the terraces. Brick, soot and strikes.

Duskwall · the wall and gatehouse checkpoint, the parade ground, the state archives, the searchlight. Stern and watched.

Clearwater · villas and the society hall on the slope, crowded terraces and the tram depot below, the harbour. Rich and poor side by side.

The journey

Travel between cities takes real time, so the journey gets its own scene. One illustration per mode (third class, first class, the night sleeper, a hired car) sits behind the journey screen, with the route, the time left, fellow travellers and, on about one trip in three, an event card.

The parcel on the seat is the first event: carry it, hand it in, or open it.

One portrait style for player avatars, patrons, NPC office-holders and Dossier subjects. The circle behind each head carries faction colour, so the same art can be recoloured if a character defects.

Propaganda

Each faction has its own period poster idiom: Vanguard monumental, Collective constructivist, Alliance civic art deco. Used on HQ screens, loading screens and city dressing when a faction dominates. Fictional symbols only.
