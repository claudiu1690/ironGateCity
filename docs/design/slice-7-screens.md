# Slice 7 — "The nation": the screens

Game designer, 3 Oct 2026. The screen-by-screen spec for slice 7: the nation map's new layer, the office pages (Governor, Chair, President), the Chair's desk, Parliament and a bill's page, the law book, the election pages for every level (the Chair, the party's pick, the republic, the result), the petition and removal flows, Clearwater's screens, the paper's new rows, the Me tab's ladder, empty states, and the phone and desktop layouts. Rules and numbers are in `docs/design/slice-7-nation.md` (cited as *design §n*). Where a slice-3 to slice-6 screen already does the job it is named and only the change is given.

**Phone first** (375 wide, supported from 360), desktop as the existing screens. **Every string a player reads is in everyday words** (GDD §1.5, design §1.1): no *Legislature*, *whip*, *Directive*, *National Control*, *recall*, *no-confidence*, *NPC*, *stand-in*, *D'Hondt*; no percentage for odds anywhere (elections are counts, and their numbers are real: a score is shown as a number, as a council total is). Review 3's lesson holds on every election screen: **say who the candidates are** (players, or the party's own figures and local deputies: townspeople run by the game), **say how the vote works in words**, and **stand the screen on its building**.

## 1. The look, and what already exists

The printed-matter look of slices 3–6. Components reused as they are: `HudBar`, `Ticket`, `Stamp`, `ResultModal` (kind `political` for every act here), `BottomSheet`, `ArtHeader` (the review-3 map crop at a point on a city picture, day or night; now also a scene), `ElectionCard`, `CandidateRow`, `VoteList`, `ResultTable` and `DistrictResultTable`, `SeatGrid`, `RuleRow` and `RuleMenu`, `OpinionBars`, `CityCard`, `OrdersPanel`, `EventCard`, `ChatPanel`, `MemberRow`, `ProfileSheet`, `HelpButton` and the help notes, `Picture`, `FactionCrest`, `Button`, `ProgressBar`, `LettersRow`, the paper's rows and front page, the residence card and sheet, `CityMap` with outlines and the quarter bar.

New components (plain props, no tRPC): `NationBar` (the republic's three shares), `LawChips`, `OfficeCard` (the Me tab's rows), `OfficePage` (the three office pages' frame), `LadderCard`, `ChairDesk` with `OrderPicker`, `PushRow`, `TargetBar`, `LineRow` (the party line) and `FixturePicker`, `ChamberHeader`, `BlocBar` (seats by party), `BillRow`, `BillPage`, `TableBillSheet` (the catalogue), `LawBook` and `LawRow`, `PledgeRow`, `NationalBallot` (three rows), `PickBallot`, `ListPanel`, `ScoreTable` (the republic's result), `PetitionCard`, `SignatureBar`, `ConfidenceBallot`, `TermLine` (the countdown to the next national date).

## 2. The nation map's new layer (route `/map/nation`; slice 4 §2)

- **`NationBar`** under the top bar, above the map (a paper strip, 44 px): the three parties' shares of the republic as one bar in the faction colours with the figures on it (*Vanguard 31.2 · Collective 35.0 · Alliance 33.8*), the kicker **WHO HOLDS THE REPUBLIC** (dotted-underlined: the help note), and at the right in Courier *the Collective leads*. Tapping the bar opens a sheet (§2.1). In transit and on every city map the bar is absent; it is the nation map's.
- **`LawChips`** under the bar: the laws in force as chips (*Eight-Hour Day Act · 9 days* …), at most five, horizontally scrolling on a phone; a tap opens the law book (§8). None: *No law in force · Parliament sits* as one chip.
- **City pins** gain a second line in Courier: *held by the Alliance* / *contested* / *close race* / *Vanguard · Fired up* (the home cities' morale word), and **Clearwater's lock goes**: *Clearwater · 25 min*, opened at Level 10 like the capital.
- **The city card** (`CityCard`) gains: a line *Governor: {name}* (or *Mayor*; *a local holds the chair* when a local does) under the opinion bars; *Held by the {faction}* / *Contested* / *Close race today* as the plate word; for Clearwater the button **Board · 20 Iron · 25 min**, and the residence line as a district's (*Register here from Rank 2 · 500 Iron*, tapping the city's residence card after arrival).
- **The Cities list** gains the plate word per row.

### 2.1 The republic sheet (`BottomSheet`, 60 dvh)

Kicker *WHO HOLDS THE REPUBLIC*; the bar at full width; a Courier line *Five cities, weighted: Irongate 40, Clearwater 30, the three home cities 10 each. Half of every party's score at the election.*; then five rows (the capital first): the city's name, its `OpinionBars`, the weight in Courier (*40*; *10 · Unrest · counts half* in the failure colour when it applies), *held by* / *contested*; under the rows **`TermLine`**: *The republic votes from {weekday} · {n} days* (or the current phase: *Names in until {until}* · *The parties pick until {until}* · *Voting open until {until}* · *The result is in: to be confirmed tomorrow*), with one button where the player has an act (**Vote now** → §7.3; **See the candidates**; **Stand for Parliament · 25 Political Capital**; **Stand for President · 100 Political Capital**). A text button **Parliament** → §6.

Desktop: the bar sits in the top bar's right half; the sheet is the side sheet's second tab (*The republic*).

## 4. The office pages (`OfficePage`; routes `/office/governor/{cityId}`, `/office/chair`, `/office/president`)

One frame: an `ArtHeader` (150 / 180 px) with the office's picture, the kicker, the holder's name in Playfair 22 px and a Courier line; then the office's **powers as rows**, each a `JobsCard`-style card with one button or one line; then the Courier footer *Pay: {n} Political Capital and {m} Party XP a day, present or not*. Anyone may open an office page (a resident sees the Governor's; a member the Chair's; everyone the President's); the buttons show only for the holder.

### 4.1 The Governor (Mayor)

- Header: the council's hall crop (the Town Hall / the City Hall / the Casino), kicker *GOVERNOR OF COALPORT · THE TOWN HALL* (*MAYOR OF IRONGATE · THE CITY HALL*), the holder's avatar at 48 px over the gradient's left, the name, Courier *since {weekday}'s result · term ends {weekday}* (a local: *{name} · Local · holds the chair; no councillor has a completed term*).
- **The Governor's rule** (card): line 1 *Which rule goes to the vote first*; line 2 Courier *Item one on the order paper, at no cost. Local councillors with no player lead vote for it. Set it by {until}.*; button **Choose the rule** (opens `RuleMenu`, eleven rows, one tap puts it; the sheet's title *The Governor's rule · at no cost*) → the modal `governorRule`. Set: the line *The Governor's rule: Public Works Order · the council votes {at}* and no button. After days 0–1: *The council voted {weekday}: {ordinance} {passed / fell}*.
- **The Address** (card): *Fifty points to the {faction} on one of the town's Issues, once a term*; two rows (the city's two Issues, each with its momentum line) and the button **Speak on {issue}** → the modal `address`. Spent: *The Address is spent for the term · {issueShort}, {weekday}*.
- **The council** (text button) → `/council`. **Last result** → the count.
- A non-holder sees the same page read-only with the holder's name and *what the Governor does* in the lines.

### 4.2 The Chair → the Chair's desk (§5)

The office page for the Chair is the desk itself; for a member who is not the Chair, `/office/chair` shows the header (the party's capital house crop: Concord House / Union House / Vanguard House; kicker *CHAIR OF THE {FACTION} · {HOUSE}*), the Chair's name and line, the standing orders as read-only rows (*Set by the Chair: {A} · {B} · {C}; the push: {place} · target {n}*), the party's rules per city, the party line on each open bill, and the Election card for Chair (§7.1) when a window is open; the button **A vote on the Chair** (Rank 3+, opens the petition flow, §9.3) when allowed.

### 4.3 The President

- Header: `scene.presidents-study` (until drawn: `scene.ministry`), kicker *PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC · THE MINISTRIES*, the holder's avatar, name, Courier *took office {date} · term ends {date} · {first / second} term*; a local: *{name} · the {faction}'s own figure · run by the game*.
- **Pledges** (`PledgeRow` ×2): the two laws pledged on the ballot, each *kept ✓ {date}* or *not yet · put it to Parliament*.
- **A law a week** (card): *Free, in a slot of your own, item one on the order paper* · button **Put a law to Parliament** (→ the catalogue sheet, §6.3) or the line *This week's law: {law} · Parliament votes {at}* / *Next: from Monday*.
- **The veto** (card): *Once a term: stop a law Parliament has passed, on its signature day* · *Unused* / *Used on {law}, {date}*; the veto button lives on the bill's page (§6.2) on the day it applies, and this card links there (*{law} is awaiting your signature today → See the bill*).
- **The Address** (card): *A hundred points on one Issue in one city, once a term* · button **Speak** (a sheet: five cities, each with its two Issues as rows; one tap) → the modal `address` with *a hundred*.
- **Parliament** (text button) → §6. **The result** → §7.4.

## 5. The Chair's desk (route `/faction/chair`; the Faction tab gains a sixth chip, **Chair**, for Rank 4 and above: the desk for the Chair, the read-only page for the rest)

A `TramBar`-style strip of four parts under the Faction header: **Orders · Lines · Events · Members** (the last two are the slice-6 tabs' views scoped to the Chair's acts).

### 5.1 Orders (`ChairDesk`)

1. **Kicker *TOMORROW'S ORDERS · STANDING UNTIL CHANGED*** with the Courier line *Three orders every member reads in the morning. The secretary writes the lines; your name goes under them. They repeat every day until you change them.*
2. **`OrderPicker` ×3** (slot A, B, C): a row with the slot's label in Oswald caps (*TALK TO VOTERS · SLOT A*), the current pick's title in body 600, the secretary's line under it in italic 13 px, and a chevron; tapping opens a `BottomSheet` listing the slot's templates (title, the line, the count: *×3*) with the current one marked; one tap picks. The rotation's pick shows as *the secretary's rotation* until the Chair sets one.
3. **`PushRow`**: a toggle *Push one order to a battleground* and, when on, two chips (*Irongate · {district}* with a district picker, *Clearwater*), a chip row for the slot (*A* · *B*), and the target chips **25 · 50 · 100** under the Courier line *The party's target: when the whole party reaches it by midnight, everyone who did at least one gets +10 Party XP. Members below Level 10 read this order in their own town.*
4. The sticky button **Set the orders** → the modal `chairOrders`. Nothing is saved until the button; the page reloads with *Set · from tomorrow*.
5. Under it, read-only: **today's orders as members see them** (the `OrdersPanel` with *Set by the Chair, {name}* and, when a push is on, the **`TargetBar`**: *The party's target today · 31 of 50 doors in Eastside* with a `ProgressBar`; the same bar shows on every member's Orders tab and the city screen's orders).

### 5.2 Lines

- **Kicker *THE PARTY LINE · PARLIAMENT***: one `LineRow` per open bill: the law's name, its line, *Parliament votes {at}* and three chips **For · Against · Free vote** (the current one inverted; one tap sets it and shows the modal `partyLine`). Under each in Courier: *{n} player deputies have voted; {m} local deputies follow your line* (or *follow the player deputies*). No open bill: *Nothing up for a vote in Parliament. The secretaries put a law up most Mondays.*
- **Kicker *THE PARTY'S RULE · THE COUNCILS***: three rows (the home city, Irongate, Clearwater): *{city} · {ordinance}* with a chevron → `RuleMenu`, one tap → the modal `partyRule`.
- **Kicker *PUT A LAW TO PARLIAMENT · ONCE A TERM · 50 POLITICAL CAPITAL***: the button → the catalogue sheet (§6.3), or the line *You put {law} to Parliament on {date}*.

### 5.3 Events

The slice-6 board for every city (not only the one the Chair stands in), with **Call an event** on each city's group (the Chair calls from anywhere; the call sheet's roles show *no role · called by the Chair*), and under the groups **`FixturePicker`**: three rows (home, Irongate with a district chip, Clearwater), each the fixture's event as a chip row (*Canvass Drive · Rally · Poster Night · {signature}*) with the rotation's choice marked *the rotation*; one tap sets tomorrow's.

### 5.4 Members

The slice-6 list with one addition for the Chair: the Officers channel chip appears in Chat for Rank 4+ (*Officers*), and the Chair's row carries the rosette *Chair*.

## 6. Parliament (route `/parliament`)

### 6.1 The chamber

- **`ChamberHeader`**: `ArtHeader` with `scene.parliament` (the painted chamber), kicker *PARLIAMENT · THE CHAMBER*, title *Parliament*, Courier *Sixty seats · {seatsV} Vanguard · {seatsC} Collective · {seatsA} Alliance · local deputies {n} of 60 · President {name}*. **`BlocBar`** under it: the sixty seats as a bar in three faction colours with the numbers on it; a tap on a bloc opens its list (§6.4).
- **`TermLine`**: *This term ends {date} · {n} days · the republic votes from {weekday}*.
- **Kicker *UP FOR A VOTE*** with the double rule and one **`BillRow`** per bill on the order paper (the President's first, marked *the President's*): the law's name in body 600; the second line Courier *put to Parliament by {name} · {party} · votes until {until} · {needed} of 60 carry it*; the third line the law's one-line text in italic 13 px; a tag when it is factional (*treats the parties differently · needs 36*) or a strike-out (*strikes out {law}*) or a removal (*a vote to remove the President · needs 40*) or an override (*vote it through anyway · needs 40*); at the right for a deputy who has not voted a radio-style pair **For · Against** (one tap selects; the sticky button commits), *your vote: For* once cast, and for everyone the running count **For {f} · Against {a}** (a chamber's votes are public: the opposite of a ballot) with the local blocs' lines in Courier under it (*Vanguard's locals: Against · Collective's: For · Alliance's: free*). Tapping the row opens the bill's page (§6.2).
- Under the rows: **Put a law to Parliament · 50 Political Capital** (a deputy with their term's bill unused; a Chair likewise) → §6.3; the President's **Put a law to Parliament · free · this week** when unused; otherwise the Courier line that says why not (*You put {law} up on {date}* / *Next: from Monday* / *Deputies and Chairs put laws to Parliament; you can vote from Rank 2 in the elections, and stand from Rank 5*).
- **Kicker *DECIDED THIS TERM***: the last ten divisions as one-line rows: *{weekday} · {law} · passed 42–18 · in force {days} days* / *fell 28–32* / *vetoed by the President* / *struck out*. A tap opens the bill's page in its resolved state.
- Text buttons: **The law book** (§8) · **The republic's result** (§7.4) · **How Parliament works** (the help note).

### 6.2 A bill's page (route `/parliament/bill/{id}`, `BillPage`)

The chamber header; the law's name in Playfair 26 px; a `JobsCard`-style **what it does** card (the one-line text, the effect line in Oswald caps with *· {days} DAYS · EVERY CITY*, the `appliesTo` line when factional: *For members of the Collective only* / *For everyone not of the Vanguard*; for a strike-out, the law struck and its days left; for a removal, *Removes {president}; {runnerUp} serves the rest of the term*); the Courier line *Put to Parliament by {name} on {date} · Parliament votes at {at} · {needed} of 60 carry it*; **the count** as a two-row table (*For* · *Against*) with each party's players and locals (*Collective: 3 players For, 17 locals For (the party line)*); the vote control for a deputy (two rows, the sticky **Vote For {law}** / **Vote Against**); for the Chair the `LineRow`; **on the signature day, for the President**: a card *Awaiting your signature · in force from tomorrow unless vetoed* with the button **Veto** (`outline`, the failure colour) → the modal `veto`; resolved: the stamp *Passed* / *Fell* / *Vetoed* / *Struck out* / *Removed* over the header and the final count.

### 6.3 Putting a law to Parliament (`TableBillSheet`, a `BottomSheet` at 80 dvh)

Kicker *PUT A LAW TO PARLIAMENT · {cost}*; when five laws are in force, a first step *Which law does it replace?* (five rows). Then the catalogue: one `LawRow` per law not in force and not failed in the last week: the name, the one-line text, the effect caps with the days, the tag *needs 36 · treats the parties differently* when it applies, and in Courier **the party lines as they stand** (*Vanguard against · Collective for · Alliance free*: the secretaries' lines, or a Chair's where set), which is the whole of the politics in one line; a second kicker *STRIKE OUT A LAW* with the laws in force as rows. One tap puts it (the sheet's title says the price; not enough dims every row with *Needs 50 Political Capital*) → the modal `tableBill`.

### 6.4 A bloc's list (a `BottomSheet`)

Kicker *{FACTION} · {n} SEATS*; **Players** first (`CandidateRow` with the avatar, *Deputy · Level n*, *first on the list*); then **Local deputies** with the fixed line *Townspeople run by the game. They vote the party line: the player deputies' majority, else the Chair's line, else the secretary's.*, the eight named (the crest mark, the name, *Local deputy · {who}*), then one row *and {n} more {faction} deputies*. The party's own figure, when President, is not in the list (the President holds no seat).

## 7. The elections

Every election screen stands on its building (`ArtHeader`): **the Chair's** on the party's capital house (the crop at Concord House / Union House / Vanguard House, day or night; kicker *CHAIR OF THE {FACTION} · {HOUSE}*); **the party's pick and the republic's vote** on **Parliament** (the crop at `irongate.parliament`, kicker *THE REPUBLIC · PARLIAMENT*); the result of the republic on `scene.election-night`. Every list says who the candidates are; every screen has the Courier line that says how it is decided and the link **How this election works**.

### 7.1 The Chair's election (routes `/faction/election`, `/faction/election/vote`, `/faction/election/result`)

The Faction tab carries an **`ElectionCard`** (the slice-3 card, kicker *ELECTION · CHAIR OF THE {FACTION}*) in these states; the same row in the paper under *Election* on the days a window is open:

| Phase · the player | Line 1 | Line 2 (Courier) | Button |
|---|---|---|---|
| Names in · below Rank 4 or no council term | Names in for Chair | Needs Rank 4 and a completed council term · closes {until} | *See who's standing* |
| Names in · eligible, not standing | Names in for Chair | Closes {until} · the secretary is on the ballot too | **Stand for Chair · 25 Political Capital** |
| Names in · standing | You're standing for Chair · backers {n} of 2 | Two backers by {until} or your name comes off · do today's orders and the branch backs you | *See the candidates* |
| Voting · not voted | Voting for Chair is open | Closes {until} · your vote is secret | **Vote now** |
| Voting · voted | You voted for {name} | Result {weekday} morning | *See the candidates* |
| Result | {name} is Chair · {your line} | Until {date} · the next election from {date} | **See the result** |
| No window | The {faction}'s Chair is {name} / The secretary runs the branch | Names in from {date} | *The Chair's page* |

**Who's standing**: the stand card (requirements as ticks: *Rank 4, Convenor* · *A council term completed* · *14 days in the party* · *2 backers by {until}*; the platform line picker; **Stand · 25 Political Capital**; Courier *Costs 25 Political Capital. You get it back only if you don't find two backers.*; the candidate card after, as slice 3's); the list under the rule *Players standing* (the `CandidateRow`s with *Back · 10 Political Capital*), then the rule **The secretary** with one row (the secretary's portrait, *Secretary Holm · keep the orders as they are · run by the game*) and the fixed line *The branch's own administrator is always on the ballot. If the secretary wins, nothing changes. A player with more votes takes the Chair.*; the Courier line under the title: *One Chair. The most support wins. Support is members' votes plus three per backer; the secretary has votes only.*

**Your vote**: `VoteList` with the players and the secretary's row; the sticky **Vote for {name}**; Courier *One vote, secret and final. The result is at {at}.*

**The result**: `ResultTable` (#, Name, Backers, Votes, Support; the secretary's row with its votes), the header line *{turnout} members voted · final*; under it *The most support took the Chair: members' votes plus three per backer. A tie goes to votes, then backers, then terms served, then who stood first.*

### 7.2 The party's pick (routes `/election/pick`, `/election/pick/vote`)

Reached from the republic sheet's button, the paper's row and the Faction tab's card on term days 21–22. Header: Parliament's crop, kicker *THE {FACTION} · {THE ACCLAMATION / THE CONGRESS / THE CONVENTION}*, title *The party's pick*, Courier *One candidate carries the party for President · one vote, secret and final · closes {until}*. The list: **Players standing** (`CandidateRow` with the two pledges as chips under the platform line, *Rank 6 · a term in Parliament ✓*), then the rule **The party's own candidate** with one row (the portrait, *Dr Konrad Albrecht · chairman of the national committee · run by the game*) and the fixed line *The party's own figure is always on the ballot. If it wins, it carries the party and holds the office if the party wins. A player with more votes carries the party instead.* The sticky **Choose {name}** → `pickVote`. Voted: the chosen row carries *your vote*. Result (day 23, on the card and in the paper): *{name} carries the {faction}*.

### 7.3 The republic's vote (routes `/election/national`, `/election/national/vote`)

- **The candidates** (`/election/national`): header Parliament, kicker *THE REPUBLIC · PARLIAMENT*, title *Who's standing for President*, Courier *Three candidates, one from each party · the republic votes from {weekday} until {until}*. **`NationalBallot`**: three rows, one per party, in order of the parties' shares of the republic: the avatar or the figure's portrait, the name, *{faction} · {rank title} · Level n* or *the {faction}'s own candidate · run by the game*, the platform line in italic, the two pledges as chips (*Pledges: Universal Care Act · Rehabilitation Act*), and at the right **Back · 10 Political Capital** for a member of that party who has a backing left (→ `nationalBack`). Under the rows the Courier line that says how it is decided: ***Each party's score mixes its share of the votes with its share of who holds the republic (the five cities, weighted), plus up to five from backers. The highest score is President; Parliament's sixty seats are shared out by the same scores.*** Then **`ListPanel`** for the member's own party: kicker *THE {FACTION}'S LIST*, the candidate for President first (*first on the list*), the list candidates in order with their backers (*Back · 10 Political Capital* when the member has a backing left; *Backed*), then *then local deputies*; the Courier line *Seats go down the list, as many as the party's score wins. Backing a name moves it up.*; the stand card **Stand for Parliament · 25 Political Capital** (Rank 5 ✓ · a Governor's term or three council terms ✓ · 14 days ✓) and, for the eligible, **Stand for President · 100 Political Capital** with the pledge picker (two of thirteen, a `BottomSheet` of `LawRow`s with ticks) and the platform line.
- **Your vote** (`/election/national/vote`): the same three rows with the radio ring; the sticky **Vote for {name}**; Courier *One vote for President, secret and final. Any candidate, any party. The result is at {at}, to be confirmed the day after.* Voted: *your vote* on the row and the line *Vote cast · the result is in {countDay}'s paper and on the republic sheet*.

### 7.4 The republic's result (route `/election/national/result`; also the front page of day 0)

Header `scene.election-night` with the kicker *THE REPUBLIC · THE RESULT* and the stamp **TO BE CONFIRMED** on day 27 (Courier *final tomorrow, once the returning officer has checked the box*) and **FINAL** from day 0. **`ScoreTable`**: three rows (the party, its candidate, *Votes* (the share), *The republic* (the control share), *Backers*, **Score**, **Seats**), the winner's row with the thin ink bar and *President* after the name; under it, in words: *Each party's score is half its share of the votes and half its share of the republic's opinion, plus its backers. The highest score is President. Seats went out by the scores: the top score takes the first seat, and so on down.* Then the three lists (who took seats, in order; the locals counted), *Turnout {n} of {m}*, and the two buttons **Parliament** · **The President's page**. The front page of day 0 is the slice-3 front page for the President and for every player deputy (the photograph, **ELECTED**, the caption naming the office, the `ScoreTable`, the CTA **To Parliament**).

## 8. The law book (route `/parliament/laws`, `LawBook`)

Header: the chamber crop, kicker *THE LAW BOOK*, title *Laws in force*, Courier *{n} of 5 · every city · the lines on your tickets*. One **`LawRow`** per law in force: the name in body 600, the effect in Oswald caps with *· {days} DAYS LEFT*, the one-line text in italic, Courier *put to Parliament by {name} · passed {weekday} {for}–{against}*; a factional law's tag; for a deputy or a Chair with a bill unused a text button *Strike it out · 50 Political Capital* (→ §6.3 with the strike-out selected). Under the five, **the catalogue** folded (*Every law the republic can pass · 13*: the thirteen `LawRow`s read-only with the party lines). Empty: *No law in force. Parliament sits; the secretaries put a law up most Mondays.*

Every ticket and desk line a law touches reads *{Law}: {effect}* in the council rule's pattern with the kicker *LAW* (*Public Assembly Act: 10 Energy* on a speech ticket; *Law: Eight-Hour Day Act +22* on the wage line); the help note on the label opens the law book.

## 9. Petitions and removals

### 9.1 A petition to dissolve the council (`PetitionCard`; the Election card and `/council/petition`)

On cycle days 0–2, for a Rank 3+ resident, the Election card's second button reads **Petition to dissolve the council · 10 Political Capital** (`outline`); while one is open, every resident's card gains a third line in Courier *A petition to dissolve the council: {n} of {needed} names by {until}* and the button **Sign · 10 Political Capital** (or *Signed*). The petition page: the hall's crop, kicker *{CITY} COUNCIL · A PETITION*, the **`SignatureBar`** (*{n} of {needed} residents*, a `ProgressBar` in the failure colour), the Courier line *If {needed} residents of any party sign by {until}, the council is dissolved: its rule is struck, the Governor's chair goes to a local, and the councillors' terms end uncompleted. Local candidates sit until {weekday}'s result.*, the signers by name (public), the button **Open it · 10 Political Capital** / **Sign · 10 Political Capital** / *Signed* / *You're a councillor; councillors can't sign*, and the line *A Town Hall event in the town adds townspeople's names: Triumph 3, Success 2, Mixed 1*.
